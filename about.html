About Me

Hi, I’m Divya.

I’m a UI/UX and interaction designer with a background in architecture, user research, and visual storytelling. I bring spatial thinking into digital product design, always keeping the people using it at the centre of every decision.

Before designing interfaces, I was trained to design spaces, studying architecture and learning how people move through, feel, and connect with their environments. That spatial way of thinking still shapes how I approach UX today, from accessibility and user flows to emotional experience and detail-driven design.

I grew up in Bhopal, India, a city known as the City of Lakes. Surrounded by water, old neighbourhoods, vernacular buildings, Mughal architecture, and a strong local culture, I became curious about how places influence people. My childhood home was a colonial-style bungalow shared with a large joint family, cousins, conversations, food, and constant activity. I had a small room where I painted, sketched, made things, and slowly understood that creativity was not just something I enjoyed. It was how I made sense of the world.

Over the years, my work has moved across architecture, teaching, design research, and digital products. Each of these experiences taught me something different about people. Architecture taught me structure and space. Teaching and mentoring taught me patience, empathy, and how to guide ideas from messy beginnings into meaningful outcomes. Today, I bring all of that into my design practice.

As a designer, I care about making digital experiences that are clear, inclusive, and genuinely useful. I enjoy working on problems where user research, accessibility, interaction design, and visual storytelling come together. For instance, in my work on Play Palooza, a mobile sports community platform, I led the UX research and interface design, creating streamlined onboarding and proximity matching flows that achieved a 40% increase in user engagement.

Outside of work, I’m still very much a creative soul. I cook big dinners for friends and friends-of-friends, volunteer when I can, and recharge through swimming, hiking, long walks, sketching by the water, or simply lying on the grass. Moving to Brisbane has felt strangely familiar in the best way, with its river, outdoor life, and relaxed rhythm reminding me of the lake city I grew up in.

At the heart of everything I do is curiosity, care, and the belief that good design should solve real problems while making people feel understood. I’m looking for a team where I can contribute meaningfully and keep growing as a designer.
