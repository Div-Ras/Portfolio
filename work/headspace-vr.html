Headspace VR

Spatial Mindfulness & User-Authored Environments in Virtual Reality (Meta Quest 3s)

Headspace VR reimagines the Headspace mindfulness application into an interactive 3D VR experience. Rather than consuming mindfulness on fixed 2D screens, users actively design and curate their own meditative sanctuaries through spatial object duplication, environmental wall colour shifting, and spatialized audio.

Concept Recap

Headspace VR explores how creative authorship and environmental control influence mindfulness, calmness, and sustained engagement. In Prototype 3, two major interaction mechanics were introduced: object duplication and wall colour change. Rather than being passive consumers of pre-set guided meditation screens, users actively design their own meditative spaces.

Core Goals & Interaction Mechanics

"It feels like I'm meditating through creation." — Usability Participant

The project focuses on four key interaction affordances inside virtual reality:

  • 1. Spatial Object Duplication: Grabbing an original 3D object (trees, animals, plants, aquatic elements) duplicates it into the user's hands, allowing them to construct custom meditative forests and spatial arrangements.
  • 2. Environmental Wall Colour Control: Pressing controller inputs ("Y" / "A" buttons) triggers real-time room atmosphere tinting (soothing cyan, light breathing pulses), matching breathing rhythms for emotional regulation.
  • 3. Theme Drawers & Spatial Audio: Physical-style furniture drawers containing music blocks/CDs that play spatialized audio loops when placed onto virtual record players.
  • 4. 3D Scaling & Discard Mechanics: Multi-touch/controller scaling of 3D objects, and a swallowing glow-ball / dustbin mechanic for deleting items.

Evaluation & Quantitative Insights

Testing Prototype 3 across multiple participants revealed a significant shift from functional interaction learning to creative freedom. Rather than asking "What does this do?", participants asked "What can I make with this?".

Improvements & Future Extensions

  • Audio-Visual Duplication Cues: Adding subtle light pulses or spatial audio chimes upon item cloning.
  • AR / Mixed Reality Passthrough: Projecting meditative virtual objects directly into users' physical living rooms for daily mindfulness.
  • Biofeedback Integration: Connecting heart rate and breathing sensors to dynamically adjust environment colors and audio loops.
