01 | Landscape Design

Landscape architecture bridges human activity with organic ecosystems. Selected projects feature human-scale retreats and public garden enhancements:

  • Farmhouse Landscape Design ("Rural Elegance Retreat"): Transforming rural acreage into a tranquil haven celebrating countryside serenity. Features structured pergolas, stone seating islands, native plant palettes, and water feature integration.
  • Doordarshan Delhi Entrance Enhancement (Prasar Bharati, Raj Rewal Site): Redesigning public arrival experiences with terraced green mounds, native flowering species, and accessible pedestrian pathways for India's national broadcaster headquarters.
  • Private Residential Gardens: North-side seating islands, firepit mounds, and sculptural plant pockets balancing private retreat with open outdoor entertainment.

State IT Technology ParkModern multi-story commercial IT hub featuring ACP curtain wall facades, entrance plazas, and sustainable daylighting.

03 | Urban Heritage & Academics

Rigorous measured drawings and urban documentation capturing traditional Indian timber architecture and sacred spatial urbanism:

  • Urban Studio — Maheshwar Documentation: Measured drawings of the historical riverside town along the Narmada River. Hand-drawn documentation of Holkar Queen Ahilyabai's palace, ornate wooden arches (Mahirap), brackets, and traditional Maheshwari saree weaver dwellings.
  • Simhastha Kumbh Mela Ujjain Study: Urban spatial research examining temporary mass gathering urbanism along the Kshipra River bathing ghats—mapping foot traffic flows, akhada zones, and transient sanitation infrastructure.
