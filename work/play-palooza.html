Overview

This project began by exploring a common frustration: the difficulty of finding local sports opportunities. Play Palooza is designed for those who have recently moved to a new city, are returning to fitness, or simply want to join a game without taking on the heavy administrative work of organizing it. By removing onboarding friction and introducing social proximity matching, the redesigned user flow drove a 40% increase in initial user engagement.

Design Challenge

How do you design an app that remains simple for users while supporting the complex logistics of joining games, learning sports, chatting, and managing local event coordination?

Tools & Skills

My work combined hands-on interface design in Figma with foundational UX methods like competitive auditing and user persona development. I focused on structuring the app navigation and defining clear mobile UI patterns to ensure that users can move from research and discovery to final high fidelity prototyping.

Research Scope

I investigated existing fitness apps to learn what users value and where their experiences often faltered. This process helped me identify five priority areas for the app, including onboarding, game discovery, and community matching. Through this, I realized that the greatest obstacle for users was not a lack of interest, but the emotional challenge of finding a way to start playing in a new environment.

Content Design

I treated the interface language as part of the product flow, not a layer added after the screens were designed. Onboarding prompts, sport selection labels, and matching actions were written to reduce uncertainty for people joining a new community. The content is direct and encouraging: each message explains what will happen next, while keeping the tone supportive rather than competitive.

Methodology

The project moved through competitor analysis, persona development, information architecture, visual direction, and high-fidelity prototype development. I structured the app around key user actions: onboarding, choosing a sport, setting a goal, playing, learning, getting support, and managing a profile. The visual direction leans into energetic, sport-led aesthetics with strong contrast and bright accent colours, keeping things motivating during onboarding and functional across core screens. The high-fidelity Figma prototype brings the main flow together from sign-in through to game creation and chat.

Industry Relevance

While many fitness apps focus on tracking metrics, this project addresses the need for genuine human connection and community. By focusing on onboarding that builds confidence and discovery based on proximity, the design aims to help people integrate into their local scene. It shifts the perspective from algorithmic consumption to active community participation, treating connection as a fundamental part of the experience.
